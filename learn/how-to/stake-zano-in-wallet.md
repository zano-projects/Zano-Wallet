**Short answer:** Keep ZANO in Zano Wallet, enable staking, and leave the wallet open and synced to earn proof-of-stake rewards.

## You'll see this if

- You hold ZANO and want rewards
- You are new to Zano's hybrid consensus

## Why it happens

Zano combines proof of work and proof of stake; stakers help secure the chain directly from their wallet.

## How to fix it

1. Install the latest Zano Wallet and sync fully.
2. Make sure your coins are unlocked and confirmed.
3. Turn on staking for the wallet.
4. Keep the computer awake and online.
5. Watch rewards arrive in your history.

Related reading: [Zano Wallet Crashes While Staking](./zano-wallet-crash-while-staking.md) and the [Zano staking explained](https://zanowallet.io/features/staking/).

## If that didn't work

If no rewards appear for a long time, check that the wallet is synced and staking is on; rewards are probabilistic. You can also read [Zano Staking Rewards Explained](./zano-staking-rewards-explained.md).

## Prevent it next time

Use a computer that can stay online; a small always-on PC works well. More on the website: [how to stake Zano](https://zanowallet.io/guides/how-to-stake-zano/).

## FAQ

**Is there a minimum?**  
Larger balances win blocks more often, but any amount can stake.

**Are coins locked?**  
Coins stay in your wallet under your control.
