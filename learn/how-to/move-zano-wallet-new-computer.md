**Short answer:** Install the latest Zano Wallet on the new machine and restore from your seed, or open a copy of your .wallet file.

## You'll see this if

- You bought a new PC or Mac
- You are switching from Windows to Linux

## Why it happens

Your wallet is defined by its seed, not by the machine.

## How to fix it

1. Back up your seed and .wallet file.
2. Install the latest Zano Wallet on the new computer.
3. Restore from seed or open the copied file.
4. Let the new machine sync.
5. Wipe the old computer once you confirm your balance.

Related reading: [Restore a Zano Wallet From Your Seed Phrase](./restore-zano-wallet-from-seed.md) and the [download for every platform](https://zanowallet.io/download/).

## If that didn't work

If the copied file will not open, use the seed; it works across every platform. You can also read [How to Back Up Zano Wallet Properly](./backup-zano-wallet.md).

## Prevent it next time

Never send your seed over chat or email to transfer it; carry it on paper. More on the website: [recovery guide](https://zanowallet.io/guides/how-to-recover-zano-wallet/).

## FAQ

**Does it work across operating systems?**  
Yes, the seed restores on Windows, macOS and Linux.

**Do I lose staking progress?**  
Staking resumes once the new wallet is synced and open.
