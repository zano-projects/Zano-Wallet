**Short answer:** No real support, wallet or airdrop will ever ask for your seed phrase.

## You'll see this if

- Support contacts you first
- A site asks you to validate or sync your wallet

## Why it happens

Your seed gives full control; scammers want nothing else.

## How to fix it

1. Never type your seed into a website.
2. Ignore unsolicited support messages.
3. Verify any staff identity in public channels.
4. Keep seeds offline.
5. Move funds if you shared your seed.

Related reading: [Avoid Fake Zano Wallet Downloads](./avoid-fake-zano-wallet-sites.md) and the [security page](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/main/learn).

## If that didn't work

If you exposed the seed, create a new wallet now and transfer everything. You can also read [How to Back Up Zano Wallet Properly](./backup-zano-wallet.md).

## Prevent it next time

Treat every request for your seed as a scam. More guides: [private wallet guide](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/main/learn).

> [!WARNING]
> Nobody from Zano Wallet will ever ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website or chat.

## FAQ

**Can admins see my balance?**  
No, Zano Wallet is self-custody.

**Are airdrops real?**  
Never if they want your seed.
