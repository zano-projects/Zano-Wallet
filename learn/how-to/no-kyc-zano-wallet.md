**Short answer:** Zano Wallet needs no account, email or ID; you hold your own keys from the first launch.

## You'll see this if

- You want a wallet without sign-up
- You value financial privacy

## Why it happens

Self-custody wallets have no company account to verify.

## How to fix it

1. Download Zano Wallet.
2. Create a wallet locally.
3. Back up your seed.
4. Receive ZANO to your address.
5. Keep control of your keys.

Related reading: [How to Buy ZANO and Move It to Your Wallet](./buy-zano-guide.md) and the [no-KYC wallet](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## If that didn't work

Exchanges may still ask for ID; the wallet does not. You can also read [How Zano Keeps Transactions Private](./zano-privacy-how-it-works.md).

## Prevent it next time

Follow local laws when buying or selling. More guides: [buy crypto without KYC](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## FAQ

**Does Zano Wallet collect data?**  
There is no account to collect.

**Is no-KYC legal?**  
Self-custody wallets are legal in most places.
