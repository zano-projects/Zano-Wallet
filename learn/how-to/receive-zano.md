**Short answer:** Copy your address or alias from Zano Wallet and share it; incoming payments appear after confirmation.

## You'll see this if

- Someone wants to pay you
- You are withdrawing from an exchange

## Why it happens

Zano uses stealth addresses, so each payment lands on a unique one-time address.

## How to fix it

1. Open Zano Wallet and go to Receive.
2. Copy your address or alias.
3. Share it with the sender.
4. Wait for confirmations.
5. Check your history.

Related reading: [How to Send ZANO Safely](./send-zano.md) and the [Zano aliases](https://zanowallet.io/features/alias/).

## If that didn't work

If funds do not appear, confirm the wallet is fully synced. You can also read [Register a Zano Alias (@name) in Zano Wallet](./register-zano-alias.md).

## Prevent it next time

Double-check the first and last characters of any pasted address. More on the website: [what is Zano](https://zanowallet.io/what-is-zano/).

## FAQ

**Can others see my balance?**  
No, Zano hides amounts and addresses on-chain.

**Do I need an alias?**  
No, but it makes sharing easier.
