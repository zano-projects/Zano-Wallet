**Short answer:** The full blockchain lives on your computer; free space on that drive or install the wallet where there is more room, and never delete your wallet file.

## You'll see this if

- Your main drive is suddenly almost full
- Windows warns about low disk space after installing the wallet

## Why it happens

A full-node wallet stores the whole chain locally for privacy, so the data grows over time.

## How to fix it

1. Check how much free space the drive has.
2. Clear other large files you no longer need.
3. If space is still short, reinstall the latest Zano Wallet on a larger drive.
4. Back up your seed phrase and wallet file first.
5. Let the wallet sync on the new drive.

Related reading: [Zano Wallet Sync Stuck at 0% or Not Moving](./zano-wallet-sync-stuck.md) and the [Zano Wallet FAQ](https://zanowallet.io/faq/).

## If that didn't work

If you cannot free space, restore your wallet from the seed on another computer with more storage. You can also read [How to Back Up Zano Wallet Properly](./backup-zano-wallet.md).

## Prevent it next time

Plan for growth: keep a comfortable margin of free space rather than running near the limit. More on the website: [download Zano Wallet](https://zanowallet.io/download/).

## FAQ

**Can I delete the blockchain folder?**  
You can, but the wallet will download it again. Never delete the .wallet file or seed backup.

**Does more space make it faster?**  
Free space prevents errors; speed depends more on disk type and connection.
