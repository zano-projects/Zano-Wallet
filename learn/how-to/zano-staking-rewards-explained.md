**Short answer:** Rewards come from winning proof-of-stake blocks; the more ZANO you stake and the longer you stay online, the more often you win.

## You'll see this if

- Rewards feel random
- You want to estimate earnings

## Why it happens

Block selection is probabilistic, so short periods vary while long periods average out.

## How to fix it

1. Stake from a fully synced wallet.
2. Stay online as much as possible.
3. Track rewards over weeks, not hours.
4. Compare against network-wide stake.
5. Reinvest by leaving rewards in the wallet.

Related reading: [How to Stake ZANO in the Desktop Wallet](./stake-zano-in-wallet.md) and the [staking overview](https://zanowallet.io/features/staking/).

## If that didn't work

If rewards stop completely, check that the wallet is synced and staking is enabled. You can also read [Zano Hybrid PoW and PoS Consensus in Plain Words](./zano-hybrid-pow-pos.md).

## Prevent it next time

Treat estimates as averages; never rely on a fixed return. More on the website: [Zano price](https://zanowallet.io/zano-price/).

## FAQ

**Is the reward guaranteed?**  
No, it depends on network stake and uptime.

**Do rewards compound?**  
Rewards in the wallet can stake too.
