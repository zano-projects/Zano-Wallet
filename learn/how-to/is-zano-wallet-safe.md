**Short answer:** Yes when downloaded from the official release, verified with SHA-256 and protected with an offline seed backup.

## You'll see this if

- You are new to Zano
- You worry about wallet scams

## Why it happens

Wallet safety depends on the software source and your own habits.

## How to fix it

1. Download from the release page.
2. Verify the checksum.
3. Back up your seed on paper.
4. Use a strong password.
5. Keep the wallet updated.

Related reading: [Avoid Fake Zano Wallet Downloads](./avoid-fake-zano-wallet-sites.md) and the [is Zano safe](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## If that didn't work

If anything seems off, stop and ask in the issue tracker. You can also read [How to Back Up Zano Wallet Properly](./backup-zano-wallet.md).

## Prevent it next time

Combine official downloads, backups and updates. More guides: [Zano Wallet security](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).
