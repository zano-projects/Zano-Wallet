**Short answer:** Copy the app to Applications, then right-click and choose Open, or approve it once under Privacy and Security.

## You'll see this if

- "Zano Wallet can't be opened"
- "Apple could not check it for malicious software"

## Why it happens

Gatekeeper blocks apps it has not seen before until you approve them explicitly.

## How to fix it

1. Make sure Zano Wallet is in Applications, not on the disk image.
2. Right-click the app and choose Open.
3. Click Open in the dialog.
4. If there is no Open button, go to System Settings, Privacy and Security.
5. Click Open Anyway next to the Zano Wallet message.

Related reading: [How to Install Zano Wallet on macOS](./install-zano-wallet-macos.md) and the [download page](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## If that didn't work

If the app crashes after opening, delete it, download the .dmg again and compare the SHA-256 before reinstalling. You can also read [How to Update Zano Wallet to the Latest Version](./update-zano-wallet.md).

## Prevent it next time

Approve the app once per new version; replace the old app when updating instead of keeping two copies. More guides: [Zano Wallet security guide](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## FAQ

**Is it safe to approve the app?**  
Only after you have confirmed the SHA-256 matches the release page.

**Do I need an admin account?**  
Approving apps requires an administrator password.
