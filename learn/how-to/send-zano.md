**Short answer:** Paste the address or @alias, enter the amount, review the fee and confirm with your password.

## You'll see this if

- You are paying someone
- You are moving coins to an exchange

## Why it happens

Transactions are irreversible, so careful checks matter more than speed.

## How to fix it

1. Make sure the wallet is synced.
2. Paste the recipient address or alias.
3. Enter the amount and review the fee.
4. Check the address once more.
5. Confirm and wait for confirmations.

Related reading: [How to Receive ZANO in Your Wallet](./receive-zano.md) and the [aliases make sending easier](https://zanowallet.io/features/alias/).

## If that didn't work

If a send fails, update the wallet and check you have unlocked balance. You can also read [Zano Transaction Pending or Not Confirmed](./zano-transaction-pending.md).

## Prevent it next time

Send a small test amount first to new recipients. More on the website: [security tips](https://zanowallet.io/security/).

## FAQ

**Can I cancel a sent transaction?**  
No, blockchain transactions are final.

**Why is part of my balance locked?**  
Recent coins need confirmations before spending.
