**Short answer:** Choose New wallet, pick a strong password, write down the seed phrase on paper, and confirm it before receiving any coins.

## You'll see this if

- You just installed Zano Wallet
- You want a separate wallet for savings

## Why it happens

The seed phrase is the only way back into your coins if your computer fails, so setup is built around backing it up.

## How to fix it

1. Open Zano Wallet and choose to create a new wallet.
2. Name it and set a strong, unique password.
3. Write the seed phrase on paper in order.
4. Confirm the words when asked.
5. Store the paper somewhere private and safe.

Related reading: [Seed Phrase Not Showing or Not Copying in Zano Wallet](./seed-phrase-not-showing-zano-wallet.md) and the [Zano Wallet security](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/main/learn).

## If that didn't work

If the seed does not display, update to the latest version and run the setup again. You can also read [How to Back Up Zano Wallet Properly](./backup-zano-wallet.md).

## Prevent it next time

Never photograph, email or type your seed into a website; keep it offline. More guides: [what is a private crypto wallet](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/main/learn).

> [!WARNING]
> Nobody from Zano Wallet will ever ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website or chat.

## FAQ

**How many words is the seed?**  
Zano Wallet seeds are a list of words shown during setup; record all of them in order.

**Can I change the password later?**  
Your password protects the file; the seed is what recovers the wallet.
