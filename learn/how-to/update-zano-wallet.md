**Short answer:** Back up your seed, download the newest installer from the release page, and install it over the old version.

## You'll see this if

- The wallet says a new version is available
- Sync stalls on an older build

## Why it happens

Network upgrades and fixes arrive in new releases; old versions can stop syncing.

## How to fix it

1. Confirm your seed backup.
2. Close Zano Wallet.
3. Download the latest installer from the release page.
4. Compare its SHA-256 with the release table.
5. Install and reopen your wallet.

Related reading: [Zano Wallet Sync Stuck at 0% or Not Moving](./zano-wallet-sync-stuck.md) and the [latest Zano Wallet download](https://zanowallet.io/download/).

## If that didn't work

If the new version will not open your file, restore from seed. You can also read [Windows Defender Flags the Zano Wallet Installer: What to Do](./windows-defender-false-positive-zano-wallet.md).

## Prevent it next time

Check the release page regularly and avoid update links sent by strangers. More on the website: [Zano Wallet review 2026](https://zanowallet.io/guides/posts/zano-wallet-review-2026/).

## FAQ

**Will I lose my coins when updating?**  
No, coins are on the blockchain and your seed restores them.

**Do I need to resync?**  
Usually not; your existing data is reused.
