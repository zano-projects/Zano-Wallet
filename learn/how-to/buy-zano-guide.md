**Short answer:** Buy ZANO on a supporting exchange or swap service, then withdraw to your own Zano Wallet address.

## You'll see this if

- You want to start holding ZANO
- You keep coins on an exchange

## Why it happens

Coins on exchanges are held by the exchange; your wallet gives you control.

## How to fix it

1. Install Zano Wallet and sync.
2. Copy your receive address.
3. Buy ZANO on an exchange or swap service.
4. Withdraw to your address.
5. Confirm the deposit in history.

Related reading: [How to Receive ZANO in Your Wallet](./receive-zano.md) and the [buy Zano](https://zanowallet.io/buy-zano/).

## If that didn't work

If a withdrawal is missing, check the transaction ID and your sync. You can also read [Using Zano Wallet Without KYC](./no-kyc-zano-wallet.md).

## Prevent it next time

Send a small test withdrawal first. More on the website: [Zano price](https://zanowallet.io/zano-price/).

## FAQ

**Where can I see the price?**  
See the price page linked below.

**Are fees high?**  
Network fees are small; exchanges set their own.
