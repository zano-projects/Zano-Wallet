**Short answer:** Zano hides senders with ring signatures, receivers with stealth addresses and amounts with confidential transactions.

## You'll see this if

- You want to know if Zano is traceable
- You compare privacy coins

## Why it happens

Public chains let anyone trace payments; Zano is private by default.

## How to fix it

1. Ring signatures blur the sender.
2. Stealth addresses hide the receiver.
3. Confidential amounts hide value.
4. Assets inherit the same privacy.
5. Your wallet decodes only your own payments.

Related reading: [Zano Confidential Assets Explained](./zano-confidential-assets.md) and the [what is Zano](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## If that didn't work

Read the CryptoNote guide for background. You can also read [Zano vs Monero: Which Privacy Wallet Fits You](./zano-vs-monero.md).

## Prevent it next time

Avoid linking your identity by reusing exchange accounts carelessly. More guides: [CryptoNote explained](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## FAQ

**Is Zano fully anonymous?**  
It is private by default; your own habits still matter.

**Is privacy optional?**  
No, all transactions are private.
