**Short answer:** Get a Zano address from Zano Wallet, point Progminer at a pool or your node, and start mining ProgPoWZ.

## You'll see this if

- You have a gaming GPU
- You want to mine ZANO directly to your wallet

## Why it happens

Zano's PoW uses ProgPoWZ, designed for GPUs.

## How to fix it

1. Install Zano Wallet and copy your address.
2. Download Progminer from its repository.
3. Choose a pool that supports Zano.
4. Start Progminer with the pool and your address.
5. Watch payouts arrive in the wallet.

Related reading: [Zano Hybrid PoW and PoS Consensus in Plain Words](./zano-hybrid-pow-pos.md) and the [Zano mining](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## If that didn't work

If hash rate is zero, update GPU drivers and check that the pool address is correct. You can also read [How to Receive ZANO in Your Wallet](./receive-zano.md).

## Prevent it next time

Keep GPU temperatures in check and use a payout address from your own wallet. More guides: [how to mine Zano](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/master/learn).

## FAQ

**CPU mining?**  
ProgPoWZ is meant for GPUs.

**Solo or pool?**  
Pools give steadier payouts for small rigs.
