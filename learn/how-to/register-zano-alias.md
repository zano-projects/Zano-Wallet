**Short answer:** Open the alias option in Zano Wallet, choose an available name and pay the small registration fee.

## You'll see this if

- Your address is too long to share
- You want a readable payment name

## Why it happens

Aliases map a short name to your address on-chain.

## How to fix it

1. Sync Zano Wallet.
2. Open alias registration.
3. Choose a name and check it is free.
4. Confirm the fee.
5. Share @yourname once confirmed.

Related reading: [How to Receive ZANO in Your Wallet](./receive-zano.md) and the [Zano alias feature](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/main/learn).

## If that didn't work

If registration fails, the name may be taken or your balance too low. You can also read [How to Send ZANO Safely](./send-zano.md).

## Prevent it next time

Choose a name you will keep; it points to your wallet. More guides: [how to use aliases](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/main/learn).

## FAQ

**Does an alias reduce privacy?**  
The alias is public, but amounts stay hidden.

**Can I transfer an alias?**  
See the alias guide for details.
