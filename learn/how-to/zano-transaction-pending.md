**Short answer:** Wait a few blocks and make sure your wallet is synced; pending transactions usually confirm on their own.

## You'll see this if

- Send shows pending for a long time
- The recipient has not seen funds

## Why it happens

Transactions confirm when included in blocks; an unsynced wallet can display stale status.

## How to fix it

1. Check your sync status.
2. Wait for several blocks.
3. Restart the wallet.
4. Update to the latest version.
5. Ask the recipient to sync their wallet.

Related reading: [How to Send ZANO Safely](./send-zano.md) and the [FAQ](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/main/learn).

## If that didn't work

If it never confirms, the wallet may release the coins back; recheck balance after a full sync. You can also read [Zano Wallet Sync Stuck at 0% or Not Moving](./zano-wallet-sync-stuck.md).

## Prevent it next time

Keep the wallet updated and avoid sending while it is still syncing. More guides: [Zano blockchain](https://github.com/zano-projects/Zano-Wallet/tree/main/learn).

## FAQ

**Did I lose the coins?**  
Unconfirmed coins stay yours.

**How long is normal?**  
Usually minutes.
